00 — PLANNING & SCHEDULING IN MINING

Planning and scheduling keep operations moving continuously.

Planning and scheduling align activities, resources and material movements over time so that the operation can work as one connected system.

But in reality, operations rarely go exactly to plan.
01 — DISRUPTION & why it matters

A vessel is delayed.
The whole plan needs to change.

The scheduler often has only minutes to recover a workable plan across several interdependencies.

FEASIBLE SCHEDULE INTERDEPENDENCIES
01
BerthCan the revised vessel sequences still work?
02
StockpileWill the required material actually be ready?
03
EquipmentAre reclaimers or trucks available?
04
Material flowCan we lock the vessel in time?
05
ConsequencesDoes the recovery create another conflict or congestion?
WHY THIS MATTERS
A plan can look acceptable on the berth chart and still fail operationally.
A decision-support tool can quickly test feasible recovery alternatives before a decision is locked in.
02 — PortOptimiser

Help schedulers recover schedules quickly after disruptions.

PortOptimiser connects operational data with scheduler and SME knowledge, translating that context into inputs the optimiser can use. Recovery alternatives are then compared against agreed KPIs.
