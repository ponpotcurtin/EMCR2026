01 — The problem & why it matters

A vessel is delayed.
The whole plan needs to change.

The scheduler often has only minutes to recover a workable plan across berth availability, vessel line-up, stockpiles, reclaiming or transfer equipment, and throughput targets.

FEASIBLE SCHEDULE INTERDEPENDENCIES
01
BerthCan the revised vessel line-up still work?
02
StockpileWill the required material actually be ready?
03
EquipmentAre reclaiming or transfer resources available?
04
Material flowCan product reach the vessel when required?
05
DownstreamDoes the recovery create another conflict or congestion?
WHY THIS MATTERS
A plan can look acceptable on the berth chart and still fail operationally.
A decision-support tool can quickly test feasible recovery alternatives before a decision is locked in.
02 — PortOptimiser

That's what we're developing with PortOptimiser.

Help schedulers recover schedules quickly after disruptions.

PortOptimiser connects operational data with scheduler and SME knowledge, translating that context into inputs the optimiser can use. Recovery alternatives are then compared against agreed KPIs.
